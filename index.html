    About the villa

    Traditional Istrian stone, modern comfort

    Villa Mišan is a lovingly restored stone house in the quiet village of Bokordići, in the green heart of Istria. Thick stone walls keep it cool in summer, while a fireplace and underfloor heating make it cosy off-season.

    Outside, a private non heated pool, sun loungers and a covered terrace with a stone barbecue look over a large fenced garden. It's an ideal base for families and friends, with sandy coves, Roman Pula, Venetian Rovinj and hilltop Motovun all within easy reach.

    — Your hosts, the Mišan family
